Google Search Console Services

Setting up proper property verification, tracking index coverage, and transforming real query impressions and clicks into monthly optimization priorities.

The Single Source of Organic Search Truth

Third-party SEO tools provide helpful estimates, but Google Search Console (GSC) is the only direct source of first-party search performance data provided by Google itself. It shows the exact search queries that trigger impressions for your site, your actual average rank positions, and real technical errors encountered by Googlebot.

I help businesses verify domain properties cleanly, submit valid XML sitemaps, and read Search Console reports to make informed decisions based on genuine user queries.

Core Reports We Inspect & Act Upon

  • Performance Report: Analyzing total clicks, impressions, average click-through rate (CTR), and average position across queries, pages, and devices.
  • Pages Indexing Report: Inspecting why certain URLs are not indexed (e.g., "Crawled - currently not indexed", "Discovered - currently not indexed", soft 404s, or canonical mismatches).
  • URL Inspection Tool: Diagnosing live HTTP response codes, rendered HTML DOM, canonical selection, and mobile crawl status for specific pages.
  • Sitemaps Report: Verifying submitted XML sitemaps, tracking discovered URLs, and ensuring crawl status remains clean.
  • Core Web Vitals Report: Monitoring field data metrics (LCP, INP, CLS) across real Chrome user sessions.
  • Links Report: Tracking external referring domains, top linked landing pages, and internal link distribution.

Turning Query Impressions into Priorities

One of the most effective uses of Search Console data is finding "striking distance" keywords. These are queries where your page already earns hundreds of impressions in positions 8 through 20. By sharpening that page's headings, answering the query directly, and adding relevant internal links, we can often lift those pages into prominent view without creating new content from scratch.

Search Console FAQ

What is the difference between Google Analytics and Google Search Console?

Google Search Console tracks what happens on Google search before someone arrives on your website (queries, impressions, CTR, and rank). Google Analytics tracks user behavior after they arrive on your pages (time on site, page views, and conversions).

What does "Crawled - currently not indexed" mean?

This status indicates Googlebot fetched and rendered the page, but Google chose not to add it to the search index. This is commonly caused by thin content, duplicate topical coverage, or lack of clear intent value.

Do you retain access to my Search Console account?

You maintain full ownership of your property. You simply grant my email address restricted or full user access during our engagement, which you can modify or revoke at any time.
