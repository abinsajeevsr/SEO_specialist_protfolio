Decision Guide

Which Service Fits My Problem?

Use this reference table to map your current search challenges to the right service.

Your Current Challenge Root Cause Recommended Service
New pages take weeks to show on Google or get ignored. Crawl budget issues, missing sitemaps, or robots blocking directives. Technical SEO
You get impressions but few searchers click your listings. Unclear title tags, weak meta descriptions, or mismatched intent. On-Page SEO
Nearby customers cannot find your physical business in Trivandrum. Inconsistent address details, lack of local citations, or weak geo signals. Local SEO
You don't know which articles to write or what your customers search for. Missing keyword research and absence of search intent mapping. Keyword Research
Valuable informational pages receive zero organic traffic. Orphan pages disconnected from internal navigation and weak link equity. Internal Linking
Competitors consistently outrank you for industry keywords. Competitor content satisfies search intent with superior depth or format. Competitor SERP Analysis
You suspect technical issues but don't know where to start. Accumulated technical debt, broken links, or redirect chains. SEO Audit

Services FAQ

Common questions about engaging SEO services.

Can I engage a single service or do they work together?

You can engage individual services based on your immediate need. For example, many clients start with an SEO Audit or Technical SEO cleanup before moving into content creation or internal linking optimization.

How do you measure progress across these services?

Progress is measured directly through Google Search Console data (crawl volume, indexed pages, total impressions, and organic clicks) and key conversion actions on your website. I do not report on vanity figures.

How do we begin working together?

We start with a direct conversation about your website, business objectives, and current organic traffic. From there, I inspect your site structure and provide a clear, prioritized scope of work.
