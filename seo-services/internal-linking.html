Internal Linking SEO Services

Connecting your pages through intentional hub-and-spoke structures to distribute link equity, resolve orphan URLs, and guide visitors through your website.

Why Internal Links Matter for Search Engines

Internal links are hyperlinks that connect one page on your domain to another page on the same domain. While external backlinks build site-wide authority, internal links determine how that authority flows throughout your pages. They are one of the most powerful, fully controllable mechanisms for search optimization.

Search engines use internal links to discover new URLs, understand the relationship between different topics, and evaluate which pages represent your core topical pillars. When internal links are structured carelessly, valuable pages become isolated "orphan" URLs that crawlers rarely visit.

The Hub-and-Spoke Topic Structure

Rather than linking pages haphazardly, I implement a structured hub-and-spoke architecture:

  • Pillar Hub: A broad, comprehensive resource that introduces a main subject (e.g., our SEO Services hub).
  • Spoke Pages: Detailed child pages that dive deeply into specific sub-topics (e.g., On-Page SEO, Technical SEO, Keyword Research).
  • Bidirectional Links: Every spoke links back to its parent hub, and related spokes link to one another with contextual anchor text.

Before & After Example: Anchor Text Optimization

Approach Link Example in Text Impact on SEO
Before (Weak) "To learn about crawlability checks, click here." Anchor text passes zero topical context to crawlers.
After (Optimized) "Follow our practical technical SEO checklist to resolve crawl barriers." Descriptive anchor text clearly communicates the target page's topic.

Auditing and Repairing Internal Link Chains

Over time, websites accumulate broken internal links, links pointing to 301 redirects, and orphaned articles. During an internal linking engagement, I audit your full link graph to update redirect targets directly, remove 404 links, and ensure no indexable page is more than three clicks away from the homepage.

Internal Linking FAQ

What is an orphan page?

An orphan page is a published page on your website that has zero internal links pointing to it from other pages. Search engine bots cannot discover orphan pages through standard crawling, and users cannot reach them without entering the exact URL.

How many internal links should a page have?

There is no rigid number. A page should include as many internal links as naturally helpful to the reader. As a baseline, every important page should receive at least 3 to 5 contextual incoming links from relevant articles or service hubs.

Should internal links use rel="nofollow"?

No. Internal links should almost never carry rel="nofollow". You want search engine bots to crawl and pass link equity freely throughout your own pages.
